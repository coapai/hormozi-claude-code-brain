# Morning Routine — The 60-Minute Stack

The morning sets the chemistry and the decisions for the next 16 hours. Get the first hour right and the rest of the day falls into place. This is a stack, not a religion — pick the parts that fit you.

---

## The minimum viable morning (15 minutes — even on bad days)

For when you have no time, energy, or motivation. Do *this* and you've already won.

1. **Don't touch the phone for the first 15 min.** No exception.
2. **Big glass of water + electrolytes** (pinch of salt, squeeze of lemon).
3. **Get outside / window open — sun on skin and eyes — 5 min.** Sets circadian clock → tonight's sleep.
4. **3 slow nasal breaths** — 4-count in, 6-count out.

That's it. Repeat for 30 days. Then add layers.

---

## The full 60-minute stack (when time allows)

### 0:00–0:05 — Wake without doomscrolling

- Alarm: not your phone, or phone face-down on the other side of the room.
- First thing: drink water that was on the nightstand. Rehydrate (you lost ~0.5L overnight).
- No social media, no news, no email for the first 30 minutes. Your brain's prefrontal cortex is still booting; cortisol is naturally high. Don't poison the chemistry with other people's emergencies.

### 0:05–0:15 — Sunlight + movement

- Step outside for 5–10 min. Even cloudy days: outdoor light = 10,000+ lux vs. 300 lux indoors.
- Move while you're out there — walk, mobility, light stretching. Body wakes faster than the mind, then drags the mind with it.
- This single block fixes:
  - Tonight's sleep (sets melatonin onset 14–16h later).
  - Morning mood (cortisol awakening response).
  - Energy curve through the day.

### 0:15–0:25 — Mind setup

Pick one:

- **Journal** (5 min): One page max. Three prompts:
  1. "If today was great, what would have happened?"
  2. "What's the *one* thing I must do?"
  3. "Who am I becoming?"
- **Meditation** (10 min): App-guided is fine. Headspace, Waking Up, Calm. Or just sit, eyes closed, count breaths to 10. Repeat.
- **Reading** (10 min): one chapter of something hard. Not news.

No phone yet.

### 0:25–0:45 — Body

Pick one based on your training program:

- Strength session (heavy days, 45–60 min — extends past this block).
- Zone 2 cardio (45–60 min easy run, bike, or walk).
- Mobility + 5-min HIIT (low-time day).
- Or simply: 50 push-ups + 50 squats + 50-second plank.

Sweat. Get the heart rate up. Don't negotiate.

### 0:45–0:60 — Fuel

- **Protein-forward breakfast.** 30–40 g protein. Eggs + Greek yogurt + fruit. Or a protein shake + banana. Or last night's dinner.
- **Coffee — but delay it 90 minutes after waking.** Adenosine clears naturally; if you caffeinate immediately, you get the afternoon crash. Wait → smoother energy curve.
- Plan the day's *one* thing (10 sec). Open your tools. Begin deep work — your brain is sharpest in the first 2–4 hours after waking. Don't waste them on email.

---

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- **Phone first.** You hand your attention to whoever wants it most, instead of choosing where it goes.
- **Skipping food until noon, then crashing.** Intermittent fasting works for some, but not at the cost of protein synthesis and morning energy. Eat protein.
- **Cold plunge as your first morning ritual when you're already stressed.** Cold is a stressor. If life is already a stressor, sauna > cold. Build up.
- **Multi-stop "perfect" 4-hour routines.** Stack so high you skip 5 days a week. Better: 15 min, done every day.
- **Caffeine before water and before sun.** You're medicating tiredness you could have prevented.

---

## How to install it

Week 1: only the **minimum viable morning** (15 min). Every day.
Week 2: add **movement block** (any form, 20 min).
Week 3: add **mind block** (journal or read).
Week 4: tune times, add fuel block.

By week 4 it's automatic. Then it's just *who you are*.
