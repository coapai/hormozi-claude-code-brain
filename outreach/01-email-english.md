# Brand cold email templates (English)

Rules: short · no price in email one · one question at the end · spec clip first

---

## Email 1 — first touch (with spec teaser)

**Subject:** `Made a 10s CGI concept for [Product Name]`

```
Hi [Name / Marketing team at [Brand]],

I'm [Name], a CGI and AI video director based in Seoul. I've made
short-form ad creative for AGE20'S, hanskin, Oroot and Arencia.

I saw your [Product Name] launch and thought the packaging would
render really well in CGI — so I built a 10-second concept before
reaching out.

▶ Watch it here: [link]

No brief, just my read on the product. Tell me the direction you
want and I'll rebuild it to your brand guidelines.

The full version runs 15–20 seconds in native 4K, includes two
rounds of revisions, and takes five business days.

Is there a campaign you're building toward this quarter?

Portfolio: [Instagram link]

Best,
[Name]
[email] · KakaoTalk: [id]
```

> Note to self: rights to the spec clip don't transfer until there's a
> contract. Don't state this in the email — only mention it, politely,
> if they ask whether they can just use the teaser as-is.

## Email 2 — day 4 (no reply)

**Subject:** `Re: Made a 10s CGI concept for [Product Name]`

```
Hi [Name],

Shot another angle on [Product Name].

▶ [link]

No pressure if the timing's off — I'll be here when it isn't.

[Name]
```

One line, one new asset, an easy exit. That's the whole email.

---

## Email 3 — day 11 (last touch of the cycle)

**Subject:** `[Brand] — results from a similar launch`

```
Hi [Name],

Last month I made the launch creative for [comparable brand]. One
15-second CGI cut did [specific number — views / saves / CTR].

▶ [link]

When [Brand]'s next launch gets scheduled, I'll have a concept ready
for it. Roughly what timeline are you looking at?

[Name]
```

Proof with numbers beats proof without numbers. Use real figures only.

## Instant DM reply script (Instagram)

When a brand DMs you, reply **within five minutes**. Never lead with price.

```
Hey — thanks for reaching out! I was actually just looking at
[Product Name].

Two quick things so I can quote you properly:

1. Which product, and what's the campaign?
2. Where will it run — organic social, paid ads, or in-store screens?

Send those over and I'll put together the right setup.
```

---

## When they ask "how much?"

Don't send a price list. Name two options, recommend one.

```
Depends mostly on where it runs. Typically:

· Campaign package — four 15–20s videos in 4K, one art direction
  across all of them, two revision rounds each, 12-month exclusive
  usage, source files included — ₩4,000,000 (~$2,850)

· Single hero video — one 15–20s 4K cut, two revisions,
  organic + paid usage for 6 months — ₩800,000 (~$570)

Most brands start with one hero, see how it performs, then move to
the package.

For [Product Name] I'd say hero is the right call. Want me to
scope that?
```

---

## When they say "that's expensive"

**Never drop the number on the spot.** Drop the scope instead.
(Closing, Rule #11)

```
Totally understand — let's find something that fits the budget.

Two ways to do that:

1. A single 6–10 second cut instead of a full hero — ₩450,000.
   Plenty for feed and stories.

2. Keep the hero but limit usage to organic only, which brings
   the number down.

Which of those works better for you?
```

---

## Tone checklist

- [ ] Product name in the subject line
- [ ] Body readable in under 30 seconds on a phone
- [ ] Spec link in the top third of the email
- [ ] No price in email one
- [ ] Ends with exactly one question
- [ ] No "I'd love the opportunity", "I'm just starting out", "hope this
      isn't a bother" — you're a vendor, not a supplicant
- [ ] Link, not a large attachment
- [ ] Named a real person if you could find one
